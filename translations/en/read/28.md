# Appendix D A Guide to Reading Existing Research

The literature cutoff is October 26, 2026. This is a relevance-led selective review, not an exhaustive systematic review or new meta-analysis. Publication date and journal name alone do not determine a claim's strength.

<a id="s2"></a>

## Well-Being and Good Lives

Start with Chapter 5's measurement distinctions, then the OECD guidelines, the Global Flourishing Study's initial report, and its 2026 multidimensional comparison.[1][24][38] Separate conceptual choices from response and living-condition data. Read criticism of normative assumptions alongside them.[45]

Group and national averages do not describe every member. Associations need not determine causes. Translation, sampling, timing, missingness, and attrition affect interpretation.

<a id="s3"></a>

## Cooperation and Institutions

Check choices, payments, and information about partners in the 2026 international cooperation experiment.[37] Applying it to joint projects requires examining differences in duration, responsibility, resources, and relationships.

Combining Ostrom's diagnostic institutional work with social-dilemma research separates feelings from incentive structures.[5][43] Avoid institutional panaceas.

<a id="s4"></a>

## Social Choice and Justification

Read Arrow's assumptions separately from conclusions.[6] Sen then shows possibilities opened by changing information used in comparison.[25]

Distinguish equality from priority, and consider arguments emphasizing justification to individuals.[32][33] This book does not claim complete unification. Compare units of evaluation and treatment of objections.

<a id="s5"></a>

## Moral Uncertainty

Moral Uncertainty discusses incorporating uncertainty about what is right into decisions.[28] Critics question combining different theories' evaluations into expected values.[29]

Read objections after proposals and ask what remains possible afterward. Neither title alone should become the final conclusion.

<a id="s6"></a>

## AI Learning and Value Aggregation

Separate performance with assistance from understanding after withdrawal in Chapter 10's learning studies.[49][50][51] Different materials, populations, and testing intervals are not simple replications of one effect.

Moving from the aggregation survey to original research, check definitions of utility and how comparisons were collected.[52] When assumptions differ, do not compare only the force of conclusions.[53][54] Keep mathematical guarantees distinct from choices of value.

<a id="s7"></a>

## AI Consciousness

Distinguish the 2026 indicator paper, internal-mechanism preprint, calibration and transfer discussion, and skeptical commentary on digital AI.[36][41][39][40]

Check systems, theoretical premises, observed functions, and inferences from functions to experience. Watch for headlines stronger than findings.

<a id="s8"></a>

## Stages of Compassion Research

Read the 2026 multilevel framework as a proposal for perspectives and measurement.[46] Naming an indicator differs from establishing validity or effectiveness. Track conceptualization, measurement development, validation, and practical effects separately.

<a id="s9"></a>

## Research on the Universe

Identify observations, assumed models, and constrained parameters. The 2026 DESI study in Chapter 19 compares observations and models in this way.[42]

Unresolved model questions do not establish cosmic will or ultimate purpose. Do not turn ignorance into evidence for another grand assertion. Use it to formulate further questions.

<a id="s10"></a>

## Keeping Reading Notes

Briefly record each source's question, method, findings, limits, and relation to this book. Record whether you examined the full text, abstract, publisher description, or only the title.

For a cited claim, inspect the relevant original passage. Do not present another paper's account as though you read the original. An objection's existence does not invalidate a study by itself; identify the dispute.

Such records let future readers and AI know what to recheck instead of inheriting this book blindly.
