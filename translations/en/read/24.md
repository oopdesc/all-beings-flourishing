# Chapter 24 Living the Next Question

<a id="s2"></a>

## Returning to the Starting Purpose

Briefly: we can choose not to treat someone's happiness as worthless because they are distant from us. Reduce avoidable severe suffering and expand conditions for choosing one's life. Cooperate toward that end, revising intelligence, institutions, and purposes.

This predicts neither fulfillment of every wish without contradiction nor discovery of a final cosmic purpose. It proposes what to protect, investigate, and revise within reality's conflicts and limits.

Concern for others does not demand self-erasure. Shared purposes do not demand identical lives. Improving intelligence does not lower less able people's worth. Cosmic inquiry does not justify postponing present suffering.

Keeping these four distinctions lets ideals protect people rather than merely mobilize them.

<a id="s3"></a>

## What Remains as Knowledge

Research measures well-being across dimensions and investigates cooperation's conditions. Mathematics shows possibilities and limits of social choice. Studies compare consciousness theories and constrain cosmic history through observables.

Important though these are, combining them does not complete a final theory. Objects, methods, and questions differ. Connecting them requires explicit intermediate premises.

I want to leave these boundaries as well as conclusions: observational findings, value choices, conditional reasoning, and unverified imagination. They show what should change as research advances.

<a id="s4"></a>

## Commitments Can Be Renewed

My purposes and convictions were not complete from the beginning. They developed through experience, thought, relationships, and language, and may change again.

Revisability does not make present commitments meaningless. I can explain current reasons and take responsibility for them, while giving future selves and others freedom to reconsider.

More capable AI and advancing research may lead to rewriting this book. Greater capability of an author alone does not make a new book correct. Checking evidence, reasoning, and consequences remains work for the next era.

<a id="s5"></a>

## Accepting Incompleteness

I cannot place here one sentence explaining everything, or honestly claim ultimate truth. There are nevertheless reasons to write.

Unwanted suffering persists; experiences go unheard; people lack real choices. Understanding can improve conditions, cooperation can make possible what one person cannot, and discovering inadequacy leaves paths for revision.

After closing the book, you need not shoulder responsibility for the entire universe. Perhaps make one question more precise, listen to someone, or rest so that you can think again tomorrow.

I wish for a world where all beings can lead lives that matter to them without destroying others. How to realize it remains unfinished. I invite readers to update questions together, rather than obey a finished doctrine.
