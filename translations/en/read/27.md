# Appendix C Designing Further Research

These are unperformed plans illustrating testable questions. Read overlapping research first and assess whether replication or extension is necessary. Inclusion here is not authorization to conduct human-subject research.

<a id="s2"></a>

## Investigating Shared-Purpose Discussion

Ask how jointly clarifying purposes, burdens, and objections affects task understanding and unequal burdens. A comparison might spend the same time explaining the same task without joint clarification. Specify what comparison defines the effect.

A possible primary measure is the proportion accurately explaining purposes and roles, with scoring rules fixed beforehand. Record actual burden distribution, access, satisfaction, and disadvantages separately.

Specify which outcomes should change over what period, not simply “improvement.” Record failure possibilities such as longer explanations with unequal burdens or reduced minority voice. Results contrary to prediction should revise the procedure or causal account.

Assess appropriate randomization, group-level assignment, and information spillovers. Design sample size with effect detection, harm, and attrition in mind. Separate primary and exploratory analyses beforehand.

<a id="s3"></a>

## Expectations of Others and Cooperation

Begin with the 2026 international experiment to examine expectations and cooperation.[37] In new settings, do not turn its conclusions into ready-made promotional claims.

Information provided must be factual and suited to the setting. Never fabricate cooperation rates to elicit desired behavior. Measure understanding, changed expectations, and changed conduct separately.

Do not rate noncooperators as morally inferior. Experimental choice, life constraints, and philosophical agreement differ. Do not repurpose data for employment or grades without consent.

<a id="s4"></a>

## Bringing Consciousness Hypotheses Closer to Research

Scientifically testing whether all subjects are one requires observations predicted differently by that hypothesis and alternatives. Without such differences, observation cannot be claimed to support it.

More concrete questions include which internal processes use representations for verbal reports, or when theory-derived indicators remain stable.[36][39][41] Distinguish functional research from establishing phenomenal experience.

Before proposing research, state not only what a finding would show but what it would still fail to show. That sentence protects the boundary between inquiry and wish.

<a id="s5"></a>

## Minimum Reporting Requirements

Report questions and prior predictions, selection and participants, comparison, measurement, duration, missingness and attrition, analysis, effect sizes and uncertainty, disadvantages, and limitations. Report changes to explanations after results.

Do not report only significant findings. Preserve unsuccessful conditions. Share materials and analyses where possible without identification; explain withheld information. Do not advertise unperformed plans as achievements.

The aim is not to determine final ethical truth in one experiment, but let others check what happens when shared purposes become actual procedures.
