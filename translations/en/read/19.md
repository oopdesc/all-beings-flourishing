# Chapter 19 Knowing the Universe and Asking About Purpose

<a id="s2"></a>

## The Observable Universe and All Existence

Understanding the universe is central to my reflections. Why is there anything? What is time? Does existence end? Large questions need not be rushed into one answer.

Contemporary cosmology studies expansion, an early hot dense state, and subsequent change through observations and models.[12] The observable cosmic history and the question of why existence exists concern different scopes.

A Big Bang model does not prove everything arose from absolute philosophical nothingness. Nor can we freely assert anything about beyond the observable universe or the earliest conditions.

Explanatory limits do not permit filling gaps with preferred answers. Separate observations, explanatory models, and unresolved conditions.

## How Research Progressed in 2026

DESI's 2026 research uses correlations in absorption by distant hydrogen and other observations to constrain expansion history and model parameters. Support depends on data combinations and dark-energy assumptions.[42]

It studies observables and comparable cosmic models, not whether the universe aims at happiness or all subjectivity is one.

What I want to learn from frontier research is not just forceful conclusions, but precision estimates, alternative explanations, systematic errors, and openness to additional observations. Such detailed work supports passion for truth.

The DESI version consulted here is a preprint. A large collaboration and precise measurements do not eliminate publication status or interpretive uncertainty.

## Does Order Defy Thermodynamics

Increasing biological or civilizational complexity may seem incompatible with increasing entropy. Yet local order and an entropy increase including surroundings can coexist.

For the second law, system boundaries matter. It does not simply prohibit order arising within part of an open system.[13] Learning and construction need not be described as rebellion against physics.

Physical order and an ethically good society also mean different things. Strictly managed societies may impair freedom and happiness. Political ideals should not be inferred directly from physical terminology.

## An Infinite Past and the Present

The thought that an infinite past would require infinite time to reach now is intuitive. That sentence alone does not mathematically prove a finite past.

Integers extend indefinitely in the negative direction yet include zero. Having no first integer differs from having no particular position. This is not a claim that real time is an integer sequence; it shows that absence of a beginning alone does not entail absence of a present.

Actual time's origin and structure require physical theory, observation, and examination of the meaning of time. Intuitions can motivate questions without determining cosmic history.

<a id="s6"></a>

## Wishing to Change Physical Laws

Wanting the currently impossible can motivate inquiry. Creating devices, revising theories of nature, and altering nature's laws themselves are different.

Aircraft fly through mechanisms operating under physical conditions, not because gravity vanished. Past technological achievements do not guarantee fulfilling logically contradictory wishes too.

I do not want ideals confined to current technology, but neither should their strength count as feasibility evidence. Distinguishing obstacles separates conditions ingenuity might alter from those we do not yet understand.

<a id="s7"></a>

## Without Knowing the Universe's Purpose

Feeling the universe alive or imagining it seeking self-knowledge can be philosophical expressions. They are not observationally established cosmic purposes.

Even if such a purpose existed, its desirability to us would be another question. Occurrence in nature does not itself dictate what is good. Distinguish what happens from what we want to protect.

Knowing the universe helps us understand possibilities and limits. Before a complete answer, we still have reasons to respond to suffering, protect freedom, and support inquiry. I want great mysteries and today's concern within one life.
