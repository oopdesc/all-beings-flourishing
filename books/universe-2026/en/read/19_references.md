# References and Scope of Reading

Numbers correspond to citations in the text. Source types distinguish peer-reviewed papers, unreviewed preprints, theoretical discussions, philosophy, and official documents. A later date alone does not imply stronger evidence. Some sources were consulted only in abstracts or relevant passages. The book paraphrases rather than quoting directly, and distinguishes source summaries from its own reflections.
