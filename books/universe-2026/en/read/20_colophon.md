How Much Can We Know About the Universe?

Tracing Fundamental Questions Through Science and Mathematics

Author: oopdesc

Production: oopdesc + AI

First English edition, October 27, 2026. Translated from the first Japanese edition of the same date.

This is the author’s second work following *Considering the Happiness of All Beings*, not a revision replacing that book under the same title. Sources were generally checked through October 27, 2026. New research may change descriptions and assessments.

<a id="h-2"></a>

## Production and Verification

Starting with the author’s questions and value judgments, generative AI was used for source discovery and comparison, text generation and editing, figure creation, translation, and file preparation. The text includes AI-generated prose and AI-generated translation. AI is not presented as a human co-researcher or a degree-holding expert.

Paper existence, bibliographic details, and relevant methods or results were checked against primary works and official materials. References identify sources checked only at abstract level or in selected passages. Not every proof in every paper was verified; no independent experiment, comprehensive raw-data reanalysis, or formal proof-assistant verification was performed. Mathematical examples are explanations with stated assumptions and small computational checks.

This book does not announce final solutions to unresolved scientific problems. It presents research, mathematical explanations, philosophical reflection, and ethical proposals according to their different grounds.
